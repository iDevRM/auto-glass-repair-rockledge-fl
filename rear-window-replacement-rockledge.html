What Does Rear Window Replacement Involve?

Most rear windows are tempered glass, which breaks into thousands of small cubes instead of cracking. The technician removes what is left of the old glass, vacuums the cargo area, seats and trim, and cleans out the frame. The new back glass is bonded or set in its gasket depending on the vehicle, then the defroster tabs, antenna leads and rear wiper are reconnected and tested.

When Do You Need Rear Window Replacement?

Back glass shattered

Tempered glass breaks all at once, so the car is open until it is replaced.

Storm or limb damage

Falling branches and flying debris often hit the back glass of a parked car.

A break-in

Thieves often break a rear or quarter window because it is out of sight.

Sliding truck window problems

Broken latches, cracked panes or leaks in a pickup slider.

Why Does Rear Window Glass Shatter Instead of Cracking?

Rear and side windows are tempered glass, made by heating the glass and cooling it quickly so the surface is under tension. That makes it strong against everyday bumps, but once it breaks, the stored stress releases all at once and the window turns into small rounded pieces. That design keeps people from being cut by large shards, but it also means back glass cannot be repaired like a windshield chip.

What Affects the Cost of Rear Window Replacement in Rockledge?

Back glass cost depends on the vehicle, the size of the glass, and what is built into it: defroster lines, a radio antenna, a third brake light or a hole for the rear wiper. Pickup sliders with power or heated panels cost more than fixed glass. Rear glass is not covered by the Florida windshield deductible rule, so your regular comprehensive deductible usually applies. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drive with a broken rear window?

You can drive short distances carefully, but the car is open to rain and theft, and loose glass can fall from the frame while driving. Tape plastic over the opening, keep valuables out of the car and book a replacement as soon as possible. Florida afternoon storms can soak a cargo area in minutes.

Do you clean up the broken glass?

Yes. Cleanup is part of every rear window job. The technician vacuums the seats, carpet, cargo area and the channels around the frame, because small cubes of tempered glass hide in trim and seat tracks.

Will my rear defroster work after replacement?

Yes. The new back glass comes with defroster lines built in, and the technician reconnects the power tabs and tests the defroster before leaving. The same goes for the rear wiper and any antenna built into the glass.

Does insurance cover rear window replacement in Florida?

Comprehensive coverage usually pays for rear glass, but the Florida rule that waives the deductible applies only to windshields. Your normal comprehensive deductible will usually apply to back glass. We can help you check your coverage before the appointment.
