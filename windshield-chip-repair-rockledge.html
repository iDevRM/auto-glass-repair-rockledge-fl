What Does Rock Chip Repair Involve?

Chip repair begins with cleaning out the break and drying any moisture trapped inside. A bridge tool is suctioned over the chip and pushes resin into every leg of the break under pressure, then pulls a vacuum to clear out air. The resin is cured with UV light, the excess is scraped flat and the top is polished. The result is a stronger windshield and a chip that is much harder to see.

When Do You Need Rock Chip Repair?

A fresh star or bullseye

The sooner the resin goes in, the cleaner the repair looks and the better it holds.

A chip smaller than a quarter

Most chips up to about this size are good candidates for resin repair.

A short crack starting from a chip

Single cracks under about six inches can often be stopped and filled.

A chip you have been ignoring

Older chips can still be repaired if they have not spread, though dirt may show more.

Why Do Rock Chips Happen So Often Around Rockledge?

Rock chips come from small stones thrown by the tires of the vehicle ahead. Commutes on I-95, US-1 and the roads feeding the newer neighborhoods toward Viera share space with dump trucks, gravel haulers and construction traffic, and a stone hitting glass at highway speed is enough to break the outer layer. Following too closely makes it more likely, and so does driving behind trucks with open beds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the chip disappear completely after repair?

A resin repair usually makes a chip much harder to see, but a faint mark often remains where the stone hit. The goal of the repair is strength first: the resin bonds the broken glass so the chip cannot spread. Chips repaired soon after they happen tend to look the best.

Can I drive right after a chip repair?

Yes. The resin is cured with UV light before the technician leaves, so the car can be driven and washed right away. There is no adhesive cure time like there is with a full windshield replacement.

How do I keep a chip from spreading until it is repaired?

Put a piece of clear tape over the chip to keep out dirt and water, park in the shade and avoid blasting the defroster or the AC straight at the glass. Slamming doors and hitting potholes hard can also spread it, so book the repair as soon as you can.

How many chips can be repaired on one windshield?

Two or three small chips can usually be repaired on the same windshield. If the glass has many chips or they sit close together in the driver’s view, a replacement may give you clearer glass.
