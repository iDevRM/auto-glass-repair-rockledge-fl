Windshield Replacement

A windshield replacement swaps the whole front glass when a crack is too long, too deep or too close to the edge to repair. It solves the safety problem a cracked windshield creates: weakened glass that may not hold the roof or the passenger airbag in a crash.

Drivers who need it are usually looking at a crack longer than a dollar bill or damage in their line of sight. Left alone, the crack keeps growing with every pothole and every hot-to-cold AC cycle, and it can earn a ticket if it blocks the view.

Call us today for windshield replacement in Rockledge. Read more about windshield replacement.

Rock Chip Repair

Rock chip repair fills a small chip with clear resin, cures it with UV light and seals the break so it cannot spread. It solves the most common glass problem on I-95: a fresh chip from road gravel.

Anyone with a chip smaller than a quarter should call quickly. Dirt and moisture settle into an open chip within days, and Florida heat turns many chips into full cracks, which means paying for a windshield instead of a repair.

Call us today for rock chip repair in Rockledge. Read more about rock chip repair.

Rear Window Replacement

Rear window replacement installs new back glass after it shatters, cracks or is broken in a break-in. It solves the open-car problem: no back glass means rain, heat and anyone walking by can get in.

SUV, truck and sedan owners all need it after a storm, a slammed hatch or a theft. Left open, the cargo area floods in the first afternoon storm and loose glass works into seats and carpet.

Call us today for rear window replacement in Rockledge. Read more about rear window replacement.

Door Glass Replacement

Door glass replacement covers side windows, quarter glass and vent windows, plus the regulator that moves the window up and down. It solves a car that will not close or lock properly.

Drivers need it after a break-in, a window that fell into the door or a regulator that failed. A side window that will not close leaves the cabin open to rain and theft, so it is not a repair to put off.

Call us today for door glass replacement in Rockledge. Read more about door glass replacement.

ADAS Camera Calibration

ADAS calibration re-aims the forward camera behind your windshield so lane-keep assist, automatic braking and adaptive cruise see the road correctly. It solves the hidden problem new glass can create: a camera that is off by a fraction of a degree.

Owners of most cars built in the last several years need it after a windshield replacement. Skip it and the safety systems may warn late, steer you the wrong way or switch off with a dashboard light.

Call us today for ADAS camera calibration in Rockledge. Read more about ADAS camera calibration.
