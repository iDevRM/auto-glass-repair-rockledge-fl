What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

A windshield replacement starts with covering the hood and dashboard, then cutting the old glass free from its urethane bond. The technician trims the old adhesive to a thin, even layer, primes any bare metal and lays down a fresh bead of urethane. The new glass is set, centered and pressed into place, and the mirror, sensors and moldings go back on. Most installs take about an hour, plus the adhesive cure time before driving.

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stabilized with resin and keep growing with every bump.

Damage in the driver’s view

Repairs leave a faint mark, so damage right in front of the driver is replaced instead.

A crack touching the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond to the frame and spread quickly in heat.

Pitting and sandblast haze

Years of highway grit make glare from low sun and oncoming headlights hard to see through.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Spread?

A windshield is two sheets of glass with a plastic layer in between, and a chip breaks the outer sheet. Every change in temperature makes the glass expand and shrink, and that stress pulls on the edges of the break. Rockledge summers are hard on glass for that reason: a car parked in the sun can have a glass surface well over 100 degrees, and cold AC on the inside sets up a sharp temperature difference. Potholes, door slams and stiff suspension finish the job.

What Affects the Cost of Windshield Replacement in Rockledge?

Windshield cost depends mostly on what is built into the glass. Rain sensors, heated wiper zones, acoustic layers, solar tint and heads-up display glass all cost more than plain glass, and a forward camera adds a calibration step. OEM glass from the car maker usually costs more than OEE glass built to the same specs. Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a windshield replacement?

Most urethane adhesives reach a safe drive-away strength in one to three hours. The exact time depends on the product, the heat and the humidity, and the technician will give you the time for your install before leaving. Driving earlier can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag relies on.

Can a windshield be replaced in the rain?

The glass and the frame must be dry for the urethane to bond, so a windshield is not installed in open rain. During summer afternoon storms we move the job into a garage, a carport or a covered parking spot, or schedule it for the morning before the storms build.

Should I wash my car after a new windshield?

Wait at least a day before a car wash, and skip high-pressure washers aimed at the edges of the glass for a couple of days. Leave a window cracked open slightly for the first day if the weather allows, which keeps pressure off the fresh seal when doors are shut.

Does a new windshield need camera calibration?

If your car has a forward-facing camera behind the mirror, the answer is usually yes. The camera has to be aimed again for the new glass so lane-keep and emergency braking work correctly. We confirm it for your vehicle when we quote the job.
